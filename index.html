Talk to a Water Expert ↗ 2-MINUTE WATER GUIDE

Water Neutrality Guidelines

Start by listing every water source on site: municipal supply, borewells, tankers, rainwater and recycled water.

Then identify where the water goes. Look at process uses, washrooms, cooling, cleaning, landscaping and discharge.

If the numbers don't add up, there may be losses, unmeasured uses or gaps in the records. A water audit helps turn those questions into a clear picture before expensive changes are made.
